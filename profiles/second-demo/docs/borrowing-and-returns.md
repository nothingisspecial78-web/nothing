# Book Borrowing, Returns & Overdue Policy

## Issuing a Loan
1. Open the **Active Loans** tab or select **Checkout** beside any available book in the catalog.
2. Enter the **Member Name** or Campus Card ID.
3. Standard loan duration is **14 days** for undergraduate students and **30 days** for faculty.

## Returning a Book & Overdue Fines
- Click **Mark Returned** on any active loan to restore the available copy count.
- Late returns incur a nominal fine of **2.00 per day** after the due date.
