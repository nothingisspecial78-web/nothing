# Campus Library Catalog Management

## Adding a Book to the Catalog
Librarians can register new books directly from the **Catalog** tab or by asking the Campus Library Assistant:
1. Click **Add Book** in the top toolbar.
2. Provide **Title**, **Author**, **ISBN-13**, **Shelf Location** (e.g., `CS-04`), and **Total Copies**.
3. Save the entry. Available copies update immediately in the live catalog grid.

## Searching and Shelf Codes
Use the search bar in the Catalog view to filter by title, author, or shelf location. Shelf prefixes follow department codes (`CS` for Computer Science, `EC` for Economics, `LIT` for Literature).
