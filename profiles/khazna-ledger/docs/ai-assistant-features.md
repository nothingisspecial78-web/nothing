# Khazna AI Assistant — Features & Sign-In Guide

## AI Assistant Capabilities & Features
Khazna AI is an embedded, role-aware assistant built directly into the campus ledger workspace:
- **Instant Ledger & Campus Queries**: Ask natural-language questions in **English or Malayalam** about student balances, pending fee dues, sector totals, batch postings, receipts, and audit history (scoped strictly to your signed-in role and organization).
- **Interactive 3D Co-Browser Guide ("Guide Me")**: Ask "Guide me how to…" and an on-screen 3D assistant and pointer highlight each step directly on the screen to help you log in, register a college, add students, collect fees, or bulk import data.
- **Cursor Element Inspector**: Click the crosshair inspector icon (or right-click any card/metric) and ask what a section shows for plain-language explanations without leaving the page.
- **Multimodal File & Receipt Analysis**: After signing in, attach screenshots, receipt photos, PDFs, Word `.docx` files, text/Markdown/JSON documents, or Excel/CSV spreadsheets directly in chat for analysis or guided bulk import validation with error reports.
- **Multi-Format Exports**: Download clean reports, student lists, pending fee summaries, and statements in **Excel (.xlsx)**, **PDF**, **CSV**, **Word (.docx)**, **JSON**, or **TXT**.
- **Voice Input**: Dictate questions using the microphone button with English and Malayalam speech transcription.
- **Organization Credits & Plans**: Every organization starts on the **Free plan** (100 monthly credits shared across the org, Admin-only access). Upgrading to **Premium** unlocks **unlimited credits** for the Admin as well as every Sector Manager, staff member, and Student in the organization.

## Logging In & Creating an Organization (Sign Up)
- **Sign In**: Choose the **Sign In** tab on `/login` and enter your institutional email and password (or pick a Demo quick-login card for College Admin, Dept Fiqh Manager, or Student).
- **Create an Organization (Create College)**: Open the **Create College** / Sign Up tab on `/login`, enter your college name, Admin name, email, and password to provision a dedicated organization workspace.
- **Password Recovery**: Use **Forgot Password?** on the sign-in form or ask your organization Admin to reset sector manager / student credentials.
- **Super Admin Login**: Platform owners sign in at `/super-admin/login`.
