# Adding and Selecting a Funding Source

## Available Funding Options
The College Finance application supports four primary funding categories:
1. **Student Fees**: Standard semester tuition, lab levies, and campus union dues paid directly by students or guardians.
2. **Charity**: External philanthropic donations and alumni scholarship pools earmarked for need-based tuition waivers.
3. **Government Grant**: State or central scholarship disbursements (such as post-matric grants) reconciled per semester.
4. **Endowment**: Recurring yield from designated college endowment trusts.

## How to Select a Funding Option
When onboarding a student or recording a ledger entry:
1. Navigate to **Funds** in the left sidebar or open the **Students** view.
2. In the **Select Funding Option** dropdown, choose one of the four active funding types (`Student Fees`, `Charity`, `Government Grant`, or `Endowment`).
3. If assigning funding to a specific **Sector** (e.g., Engineering or Arts & Science), verify that the fund's **Target Sector** matches or is set to `All Sectors`.
4. Click **Add Fund Source** to register a new funding pool with an initial allocation amount and fiscal year.
