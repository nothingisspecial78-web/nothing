# Charity Funds & Scholarship Disbursements

## Managing Charity Funding Pools
Charity funds come from alumni trusts, community foundations, and emergency student relief drives. Unlike general `Student Fees`, every **Charity** fund entry requires an explicit **Donor / Source Name**, an **Allocation Amount**, and trustee **Audit** tracking.

## Assigning Charity Support to Students
1. Ensure an active fund of type `Charity` exists in the **Funds** tab with sufficient remaining balance.
2. When importing students via Excel/CSV, set the **Funding Option** column to `Charity`.
3. Alternatively, update an individual student's funding option to `Charity` during onboarding.

## Audit & Compliance
All charity fund additions and student waivers executed via the AI Assistant are recorded in the **Action Audit Log** with the initiating user ID, timestamp, and affected record IDs for annual trustee audits.
