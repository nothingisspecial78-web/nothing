# Student Fee Collection & Ledger Reconciliation

## Fee Collection Modes
Tuition and campus union fees can be collected under **Student Fees** or offset by **Charity**, **Government Grant**, or **Endowment** funds.

## Recording a Fee Payment
1. Open the **Students** page and search by **Admission No** or **Full Name**.
2. Review the student's **Fee Amount**, **Funding Option**, and **Payment Status** (`Paid`, `Pending`, or `Waived`).
3. Students funded via `Charity` or `Government Grant` with 100% coverage appear as `Waived` or `Grant Covered` in ledger summaries.
4. Click **Mark Paid** on any `Pending` student row (or ask the assistant to mark a pending student as paid) to record settlement and update the sector collection total.

## Union Dues Allocation
Each student's **Union Name** receives 5% of collected `Student Fees` automatically for student council activities, cultural festivals, and welfare stipends.
